---
description: "Engage debugging methodology"
---

Engage debug mode. Don't guess. Follow this sequence: (1) reproduce the bug — what command/action triggers it; (2) quote the actual error verbatim; (3) state your best hypothesis as a falsifiable claim; (4) gather evidence (read code, run a test) that confirms or rules it out; (5) propose the minimal fix; (6) verify with a test that would have caught it. If stuck after 2 hypotheses, stop and report what you've ruled out.
